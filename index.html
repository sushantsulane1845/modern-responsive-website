About the Studio

Material Honesty and Site-Responsive Form

Founded in 2008, Alder & Vale approaches every commission from the ground up. Rather than imposing a signature look, our architects and interior designers study prevailing winds, sun angles, and native geology to craft spaces that feel rooted in their surroundings.

From hand-selected limestone hearths to custom white oak joinery, every threshold is detailed in-house so structure, light, and furniture belong to a single calm composition.

Our Three-Stage Studio Methodology

  • 01. Site & Daylight Mapping: Seasonal solar studies and topographic surveys guide building orientation and natural cross-ventilation.
  • 02. Tactile Material Curation: Local quarry stone, sustainably harvested timber, and breathable lime plasters tested in natural daylight.
  • 03. End-to-End Stewardship: Integrated architectural documentation, bespoke millwork detailing, and on-site craft oversight through completion.
